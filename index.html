TECHNIQUE 01 · GROUPING

Look for structure
before doing arithmetic.

A repeating pattern can tell you how to organize your work. Pause, look at the signs, and choose a small first step.

01

Keep the sign attached.

When you regroup, a negative term stays negative. Moving −6 does not turn it into +6.

02

Ask why it works.

A shortcut becomes a skill when you can explain why it preserves the original calculation.

03

Give it another try.

After reading a solution, come back later and work through the same problem without the steps.
